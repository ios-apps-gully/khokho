Kho-Kho

A chasing game from South Asia. Seven stages, one chaser, three runners.

iPhone & iPad No accounts No in-app purchases Ads between stages only

How it plays

  • Drag anywhere and the active chaser follows your finger. Lift your finger and they stop.
  • Run into a runner to catch them. Catch all three and the stage is yours.
  • Run into a seated team-mate and the kho passes: they jump up, you sit down, and the chase carries on from where they were facing.
  • Do not touch the yellow centre line between the poles. Four touches and the runners take the stage. You may cross around either end pole — those are the free zones.
  • Each catch earns the chaser 30 seconds of extra speed.

Ads

There is one full-screen advertisement between stages, never during play. Ads are capped at a General audiences rating and are non-personalised — see the privacy policy for what that means in practice.

Something wrong? A suggestion?

Open an issue at github.com/ios-apps-gully/khokho/issues. If it is a bug, the most useful things to include are your device, the iOS version, and which stage you were on.

Play it for real

Kho-Kho needs no equipment — chalk a line, pick teams, and go. If this app gets one afternoon of it started in a park, it has done its job.

Privacy policy

Kho-Kho is an independent game about a street game from South Asia. It is not affiliated with any sporting federation.
