Privacy Policy — Kho-Kho

Last updated 18 October 2026

Kho-Kho is a game. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no profiles and no leaderboards. We do not ask you for your name, your email address or anything else about you, and there is no server of ours for any of it to go to.

What stays on your device

The app remembers two settings, stored on the device only:

  • which neighbourhood backdrop you last chose;
  • whether you have the Karachi backdrop switched on.

They never leave your phone, and deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising

The app shows one full-screen advertisement between stages, supplied by Google AdMob. To do that, Google receives and processes technical information from your device — typically a device advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, and data about how the ad was shown and interacted with.

Ads in this app are set up for children. We send Google the child-directed and under-age-of-consent signals on every single request, cap the ad content rating at G (General audiences), and ask for non-personalised ads only. That means ads are not selected using a profile of your behaviour, and creatives rated above G — which is where suggestive imagery and strong language sit — are excluded.

Google's handling of that data is governed by its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking of our own.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites. The app does not ask for permission to track, because it does not.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone, because nothing is collected.

Children

This app is suitable for children and is built on that basis. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age. If you believe a child has somehow provided personal information through this app, raise it at the link below and it will be dealt with.

Changes

If the app ever collects something it does not collect today, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about the app: open an issue at github.com/ios-apps-gully/khokho/issues.

Kho-Kho is an independent game about a street game from South Asia. It is not affiliated with any sporting federation.
